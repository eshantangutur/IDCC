# Argument Forensics
### Beyond the Speech. Into the Structure of the Argument.

**Argument Forensics is an AI-powered debate intelligence platform that deconstructs competitive debate speeches into their underlying argumentative structures, exposing the reasoning, assumptions, evidence, and logical connections that determine whether an argument actually holds up.**

Debate is more than the exchange of claims. Every compelling argument relies on a complex network of warrants, evidence, assumptions, impacts, and logical relationships. Yet, competitors often struggle to identify precisely where an argument succeeds, where its reasoning breaks down, and what an opponent needs to challenge to dismantle it.

Argument Forensics addresses this gap by transforming unstructured debate speeches into structured, actionable argument analysis.

## The Problem

Most debate preparation tools focus on generating arguments, summarizing evidence, or producing generalized feedback. However, these approaches often overlook the most important part of competitive debate: **the internal architecture of an argument.**

A claim might sound persuasive while relying on an unsupported warrant. An impact might be compelling while lacking a defensible causal link. A piece of evidence might be relevant without actually proving the conclusion attached to it.

Recognizing these weaknesses requires more than summarization. It requires reconstructing how an argument works.

## The Solution

Argument Forensics approaches debate analysis as a process of argumentative reconstruction.

Rather than simply telling competitors whether a speech is good or bad, the platform examines how its arguments are constructed, identifies the relationships between their components, and highlights weaknesses that might otherwise go unnoticed.

### Core Capabilities

- **Argument Reconstruction:** Break speeches into their fundamental components, including claims, evidence, warrants, reasoning, and impacts.
- **Logical Analysis:** Identify unsupported assumptions, missing warrants, reasoning gaps, and potential logical fallacies.
- **Argument Mapping:** Reveal how individual claims, supporting evidence, and conclusions connect to form larger argumentative structures.
- **Vulnerability Detection:** Highlight areas where opposing competitors can challenge an argument, question its assumptions, or expose weaknesses in its reasoning.
- **Actionable Feedback:** Translate complex analytical findings into practical insights that competitors can use to strengthen speeches and improve rebuttals.
- **Debate Format Adaptability:** Support the distinct analytical needs of competitive formats such as Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, Congressional Debate, and World Schools.

## More Than an AI Chatbot

Argument Forensics is designed around a fundamental distinction: **generating a response is not the same as understanding an argument.**

A conventional chatbot can summarize a speech or provide general feedback. Argument Forensics instead focuses on the underlying structure of the reasoning itself.

Its objective is to move beyond conversational responses toward systematic argument analysis, making the relationships between claims, warrants, evidence, and impacts explicit and inspectable.

The result is a tool built around the actual intellectual demands of competitive debate.

## Who Is It For?

- **Competitive Debaters:** Identify weaknesses in cases, improve argument construction, and prepare more effective rebuttals.
- **Debate Coaches:** Examine student arguments and provide more targeted feedback.
- **Speech and Debate Teams:** Develop stronger analytical habits and improve the quality of competitive preparation.
- **Students and Researchers:** Better understand argumentation, critical thinking, and logical reasoning.

## Vision

The goal of Argument Forensics is not to replace the critical thinking that makes debate valuable. It is to make that thinking more visible, understandable, and accessible.

By combining artificial intelligence with structured argumentative analysis, Argument Forensics aims to help competitors move beyond memorizing arguments and toward understanding exactly why those arguments work.

**Because winning an argument starts with understanding how it is built.**
